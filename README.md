# Duke Energy Bill Pay Guide

An independent informational guide that helps readers find official Duke Energy billing, payment, account, assistance, troubleshooting, and scam-awareness resources.

## Website

https://getlivesupport.github.io/dukeenerguide/

## Important disclosure

This website is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Duke Energy. It does not process utility payments, access Duke Energy accounts, or collect usernames, passwords, verification codes, bank details, or utility account numbers.

For billing and account matters, use the official Duke Energy website or the contact information printed on the user's current bill.

## Contact

For general questions about navigating this guide, contact Live Support at [1 (888) 414-4406](tel:+18884144406). Live Support cannot access Duke Energy accounts or process payments.
