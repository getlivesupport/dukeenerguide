How to Pay Your Duke Energy Bill Online

Online payment is usually the fastest way to review your account, see your balance, and submit a payment. You can use a computer, tablet, or mobile device.

Visit the official Duke Energy website

Go directly to Duke Energy Billing and Payment Options or use the My Account page.

Sign in to My Account

Enter your username and password. If you do not have an online profile, select the registration option and follow the prompts.

Review your bill

Confirm the account number, billing amount, due date, and any past-due balance before submitting payment.

Choose a payment method

Select the payment method available for your location, such as a bank account or another option displayed in your account.

Submit and save confirmation

Submit the payment and save the confirmation number or email. Check your account later to verify that the payment posted.

Tip: Duke Energy’s My Account tools can also provide billing history, usage information, payment confirmations, account notifications, and payment-plan features when available for your location.

How to Set Up Duke Energy AutoPay

AutoPay can help prevent missed due dates by automatically scheduling recurring payments from an eligible payment method.

  • Sign in to your official Duke Energy My Account.
  • Open the billing, payment, or AutoPay section.
  • Choose the payment method you want to use.
  • Review the scheduled payment date and terms.
  • Confirm enrollment and save the confirmation message.
  • Keep enough funds available before each scheduled payment.

AutoPay availability, payment timing, and eligible payment methods can vary depending on your Duke Energy service territory.

Other Ways to Pay Your Duke Energy Bill

If you do not want to pay online, Duke Energy may provide several additional payment methods. Use the instructions on your bill or confirm the available options for your state.

1

Pay by phone

Call the customer-service or payment number printed on your latest bill. Follow the automated instructions and keep your confirmation number.

2

Pay by mail

Send a check or money order using the payment address printed on your bill. Include the payment stub when requested and allow enough time for delivery.

3

Pay in person

Use an authorized payment location listed by Duke Energy. Verify the location and accepted payment methods before visiting.

4

Use your bank

You may be able to schedule an online bill payment through your bank. Use the correct Duke Energy account number and allow time for bank processing.

Duke Energy Bill Payment Assistance

If you are having difficulty paying your bill, contact Duke Energy as early as possible. Assistance programs and eligibility rules depend on your state, income, account status, and service territory.

Payment arrangements

Sign in to My Account or contact Duke Energy to check whether an installment plan, due-date extension, or other arrangement is available.

Payment Assistance Finder

Some customers can use Duke Energy’s assistance resources to search for local organizations and programs by ZIP code.

Budget or equal payment plans

Eligible customers may have access to plans that spread expected energy costs across more consistent monthly payments.

Act early: Do not wait until the due date or a disconnection notice if you already know you may have trouble paying. Contact Duke Energy using the number on your bill or the official website.

Duke Energy Bill Pay Troubleshooting

Payment failed

Check that your payment details are correct, your account has sufficient funds, and your bank has not blocked the transaction. Do not submit repeated payments without checking your account.

Payment is pending

Review the confirmation email or number and allow processing time. Contact Duke Energy if the payment does not appear after the expected processing period.

Cannot sign in

Use the official password-reset option. Avoid entering your login information on links received in unexpected texts or emails.

Wrong payment amount

Compare the payment amount with your current bill and account balance. Contact Duke Energy if a duplicate or incorrect payment was submitted.

How to Avoid Duke Energy Payment Scams

Watch for urgent payment demands

Be cautious if someone demands immediate payment, threatens disconnection, asks for gift cards or cryptocurrency, or tells you to use an unusual payment method. Do not use a phone number or payment link supplied by a suspicious caller or message.

Verify your account by visiting the official Duke Energy website directly or calling the number printed on your bill.

  • Do not share your password, verification code, or full banking details.
  • Do not purchase gift cards to pay an energy bill.
  • Do not click suspicious payment links in texts or emails.
  • Use the official Duke Energy website rather than a search-ad link when possible.
  • Keep payment confirmations and report suspicious activity promptly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I pay my Duke Energy bill online?

Use the official Duke Energy Billing and Payment Options page or sign in through the official My Account page.

Can I pay my Duke Energy bill without signing in?

Payment options may vary by state and account type. Visit the official billing page to see which options are currently available for your service territory.

How do I know whether my payment went through?

Save the confirmation number or email after submitting payment. You can also sign in to My Account to review your payment activity.

What should I do if I cannot afford my bill?

Contact Duke Energy as soon as possible and ask about payment arrangements, assistance programs, or other options available in your area.

Can I change or cancel AutoPay?

Sign in to My Account and open the payment or AutoPay settings. Review the account instructions carefully before changing a scheduled payment.
